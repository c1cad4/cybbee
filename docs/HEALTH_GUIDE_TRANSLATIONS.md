# Detailed translations: queen introduction, Varroa and oxalic acid

## Delivered

The core guides for queen introduction, Varroa monitoring/management and oxalic-acid label planning now include introductions, actions, two explanatory sections, two decision scenarios, checklists and FAQ in Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Bengali, French and German. Russian and English were already available. These are translations of CybBee's editorial core guides, not translations of entire external publisher articles or product labels.

Together with wintering, four core topics are detailed in all ten languages. All thirteen core topics remain detailed in Russian/English. Other long topics and some additional editorial supplements still need translation into the remaining eight languages. The original Russian content is retained, explicitly labelled and collapsed.

The Settings and Knowledge overview now calculate translation coverage from actual delivered articles. Cards distinguish detailed and concise guides. Coverage is not a promise of native-speaker or veterinary review.
